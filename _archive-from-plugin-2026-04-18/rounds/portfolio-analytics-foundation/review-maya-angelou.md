# Maya Angelou Review — Compass Copy

## Verdict
**Too much machinery, not enough music.**

Language feels like documentation—clean, clear, corporate. Rhythm exists but it's marching, not breathing. Headlines inform but don't stop you. Nothing tries too hard, but nothing sings either.

---

## Does it feel human?
**Mostly no.**

### What works:
- "Compass is a truth machine" — direct, clear
- "Pinned is dying. 62% never returned. Kill it or pivot." — brutal, real
- "Relief from certainty (not anxiety from guessing)" — rhythm here

### What doesn't:
- "Build foundational analytics infrastructure that enables data-driven decisions, cross-product learning, and the compounding advantages that separate portfolios from product collections." — This is LinkedIn prose. Nobody talks like this.
- "Validates Elon's SQLite claim" — reference without context feels insider-y
- Too many parentheticals break intimacy: "(per decisions.md line 541)" — footnotes kill feeling

---

## Is there rhythm?
**Barely.**

Some lines have it:
- "The truth. The future. The value."
- "Relief from certainty"
- "Dead. Decision: Kill or pivot."

Most lines are flat:
- "SQLite handles 100k writes/day single-threaded"
- "4-hour migration to Postgres when actually needed"
- Long sentences with commas pile up: "SQLite database with events table (6 columns), WAL mode enabled, Timestamp index for performance, Auto-vacuum enabled"

Lists kill rhythm. They organize but don't move.

---

## Do headlines stop you?
**No.**

Headlines announce, don't grab:
- "Architecture: SQLite Over PostHog" — serviceable
- "Privacy-First Approach" — expected
- "V1 Scope: Ruthless Cuts" — better, but still polite

What would stop me:
- "SQLite. Because complexity is the real cost."
- "No dashboard. Just truth in your inbox."
- "Kill it or ship it. No in-between."

---

## Is anything trying too hard?
**No.**

This is the opposite problem. It's buttoned up. Safe. The "brutal honesty" voice is promised but not delivered—except in examples.

The spec talks *about* being direct more than it *is* direct.

---

## Three weakest lines rewritten

### 1. Original:
"Build foundational analytics infrastructure that enables data-driven decisions, cross-product learning, and the compounding advantages that separate portfolios from product collections."

### Rewritten:
"Know which product is dying before you waste another month on it."

---

### 2. Original:
"SQLite handles 100k writes/day single-threaded. $0 infrastructure cost. 4-hour migration to Postgres when actually needed. No infrastructure complexity."

### Rewritten:
"SQLite handles 100k writes before breakfast. Costs nothing. If you outgrow it? Four hours to Postgres. Until then, build."

---

### 3. Original:
"Wave 1 (Foundation): ⬜ 0/30 tasks complete
Wave 2 (Backend): ⬜ 0/40 tasks complete
Wave 3 (Integration): ⬜ 0/42 tasks complete
Wave 4 (Validation): ⬜ 0/23 tasks complete"

### Rewritten:
"Foundation. Backend. Integration. Validation. 135 tasks between you and truth."

---

## Final note
You've built a machine that works. Now give it a voice people remember.
