# Steve Jobs — Portfolio Analytics Foundation

## Product Naming: "Compass"

One word. Inevitable. **Compass.**

Not "Portfolio Analytics Dashboard" or "Product Insights Platform." Those are committee names. Boring. Forgettable.

A compass shows you where you are and where you're going. It's essential. It's trusted. It doesn't lie. When you're lost at sea with five products pulling in different directions, you need a compass. Simple. Elegant. Done.

## Design Philosophy: Ruthless Clarity

This product must be **insanely simple**. I'm looking at this PRD and I see seventeen features, six products, dozens of metrics. That's not a product. That's a spreadsheet with anxiety.

Here's the truth: **You can only look at three numbers at a time.** That's it. The human brain can't process dashboards with 47 metrics. So we're going to build a product that shows you the **three numbers that matter most** — right now, this week, for your portfolio.

Everything else? Two taps away. Hidden. Waiting for when you need it. But the first screen? Three numbers. Big. Bold. Undeniable.

Like the original iPod: "1,000 songs in your pocket." Not "MP3 player with 5GB storage and USB 2.0 connectivity." One sentence. One promise.

## User Experience: The First 30 Seconds

You open Compass. No loading spinner. No tutorial. No "Welcome to your analytics journey!"

**You see:**
- **One big number:** Your portfolio's heartbeat. Daily active users. Growing or dying. Green or red.
- **One trend line:** The last 30 days. Is this going up and to the right, or are you in trouble?
- **One insight:** "LocalGenius retention dropped 23% this week. Investigate."

That's it. **Thirty seconds. Three things. One decision.**

If you want more, you tap. You drill down. You explore. But the default experience is *clarity*, not *complexity*. Most analytics tools are built by engineers who think more data = more value. Wrong. More data = more confusion.

We're going to build the analytics tool that **respects your time**.

## Brand Voice: The Truth-Teller

Compass doesn't sugarcoat. It doesn't say "Your retention is suboptimal" or "Consider investigating user engagement patterns."

It says: **"Pinned is dying. Fix it or shut it down."**

This is the analytics tool that tells you what you need to hear, not what you want to hear. It's your board member who actually cares. It's Warren Buffett looking at your numbers and saying, "This doesn't make sense."

The voice is:
- **Direct:** No jargon. No corporate-speak.
- **Confident:** This isn't "data suggests" — it's "here's what's happening."
- **Actionable:** Every insight ends with "Do this."

Analytics tools are full of insights that go nowhere. Compass tells you what to do next.

## What to Say NO To

I'm looking at this feature list and I want to cut half of it.

**NO to:**
- ❌ **Cohort analysis in V1.** You don't have enough users yet. Build it when it matters.
- ❌ **Time range selectors.** Default to 30 days. That's the only time range that matters for a portfolio this size. You can add custom ranges in V2 if someone actually asks for them. (They won't.)
- ❌ **Churn prediction models.** You want to build an ML model with 50 users? Come on. Track churn. Show churn. Predict churn later.
- ❌ **Weekly digest emails.** Nobody reads these. If something is important, show it in the app. If it's not important, why are you sending it?
- ❌ **Board member access.** You're building a portfolio tool, not a board reporting tool. Different products. Don't confuse them.

Every feature you add is a feature you have to maintain, explain, and support. **Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication.** Cut the features that don't serve the core promise: *Know your portfolio's health in 30 seconds.*

## The Emotional Hook: Control

Right now, you're flying blind. You have five products and you don't know which one is winning. That feeling? It's not just frustrating. It's **terrifying**.

You're making bets without data. You're spending time on the wrong product. You're missing the signals that could 10x your portfolio.

Compass gives you **control**. Not the illusion of control — actual control. You open it, you see the truth, you make a decision. No guesswork. No hoping your instinct is right.

That feeling when you look at your bank account after getting paid? When the number is bigger than you expected? That tiny rush of "everything's okay"? **That's what Compass feels like every morning.**

You'll open it before your first coffee. Not because you *have* to. Because you *want* to. Because knowing is better than guessing.

## The Bar We're Setting

I don't want this to be "good enough." I don't want users to tolerate it because it's better than nothing.

I want this to be the analytics tool that makes every other tool feel bloated, confusing, and slow. I want people to look at PostHog or Mixpanel or Amplitude and think, "Why is this so complicated?"

**Compass is analytics for people who hate analytics tools.**

Build that, and you'll have something insanely great.
