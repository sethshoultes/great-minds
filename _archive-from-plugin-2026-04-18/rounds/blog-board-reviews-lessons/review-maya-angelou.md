# Maya Angelou Review: Copy & Messaging

## Does the language feel human?

**Yes—mostly.**

- "Honey, this isn't a product. This is plumbing." ✓
- "That's not a statistic—that's forty-eight moments where trust eroded." ✓
- "The experience of 'things just working' is itself a welcome." ✓

**But some lines reach:**
- "The best businesses don't sell products. They solve loneliness."
- "That's a hand extended in the dark."

Oprah's quotes feel like Oprah. The others? Sometimes they're performing themselves.

## Is there rhythm?

**Strong rhythm appears in:**
- Jensen's triplets: "Static doesn't compound."
- Buffett's folksy inversions: "This is a cost center disguised as a deliverable."
- Shonda's beats: "No story arc... it has a moment. One moment."

**Dead rhythm in analysis sections:**
- "Quantified frequency: 460+ occurrences across reviews"
- "~95% of reviews (est. 165+/175)"

Numbers kill rhythm. Let the quotes breathe.

## Does the headline stop you?

**No.**

"Board Review Pattern Analysis" sounds like a quarterly report.

You analyzed 175 reviews. Jensen asked about moats 460 times. Lead with that.

## Is anything trying too hard?

**Yes.**

- "The Moat Obsessive" (too on-the-nose)
- "Lab Report with Soul" (Steve Jobs' requirement)
- "That's not just a tagline. That's a hand extended in the dark."

When you announce the soul, you kill it.

## The 3 weakest lines—rewritten

### 1. WEAK
> "Board Review Pattern Analysis"

**REWRITE**
> "We asked Jensen about moats 175 times. He said the same thing every time."

---

### 2. WEAK
> "~95% of reviews (est. 165+/175) include explicit moat analysis"

**REWRITE**
> "Jensen found a way to talk about moats in every review. Even the one about CSS."

---

### 3. WEAK
> "The best businesses don't sell products. They solve loneliness. When a small business owner launches their site and lies awake wondering 'is it working?', they're experiencing a kind of loneliness. Anchor says: 'Someone's got your back.' That's not just a tagline. That's a hand extended in the dark."

**REWRITE**
> "You launch. You lie awake. Is it working? That's loneliness. Anchor says: Someone's got your back."

---

## Final note

You have 50 great quotes. Let them do the work. Stop framing them. Stop counting them. Stop explaining why they matter.

Put the quote on the page. Move on.
