# Maya Angelou Review: WP Autopilot Copy

**Verdict: Mostly human. Rhythm exists. A few lines reach. Three need rewriting.**

---

## Does the language feel human, not corporate?

**Yes, mostly.**

- "WordPress without the manual" — Real voice.
- "What would you like to build?" — Feels like a friend asking.
- "Your API key is configured and encrypted" — Gets technical without sounding robotic.

**But:**
- "Chat with your site to create pages, manage plugins, and configure content through natural language powered by Claude AI" — Too many clauses. Loses breath.

---

## Is there rhythm in the sentences?

**Some.**

- Short greeting: "What would you like to build?" (5 words)
- Example prompt flows naturally: "Try: 'Create a contact page...'"

**Missing:**
- Settings copy alternates between choppy ("Enter your Claude API key from Anthropic") and run-on ("Chat with your site to...").
- No cadence. All medium length.

---

## Does the headline stop you?

**"What would you like to build?"**

Yes. Direct. Inviting. Makes you pause.

**"WordPress without the manual."**

Yes. Promise in six words.

**"WP Autopilot Settings"**

No. Generic admin speak.

---

## Is anything trying too hard?

**Not really.**

- No exclamation marks!!!
- No "revolutionary" or "game-changing."
- No hype.

**One close call:**
- "API key configured! You're ready to use WP Autopilot" — The exclamation feels tacked on. Success doesn't need cheerleading.

---

## The 3 Weakest Lines (Rewritten)

### 1. **Plugin Description**

**Current:**
"Chat with your site to create pages, manage plugins, and configure content through natural language powered by Claude AI."

**Why it fails:**
Too many ideas in one breath. Sounds like a feature list, not a feeling.

**Rewrite:**
"Talk to your site. It listens. Pages appear. Plugins install. No manual required."

---

### 2. **API Key Placeholder**

**Current:**
"sk-ant-api03-AbCdEfGhIjKlMnOpQrStUvWxYz1234567890AbCdEfGhIjKlMnOpQrStUvWx"

**Why it fails:**
Intimidating wall of characters. Feels like code, not an invitation.

**Rewrite:**
"sk-ant-api03-..." (with lighter gray)

---

### 3. **Setup Success Message**

**Current:**
"API key configured! You're ready to use WP Autopilot."

**Why it fails:**
The exclamation adds false enthusiasm. "Ready to use" is passive.

**Rewrite:**
"You're in. Go build something."

---

## What Works (Don't Touch)

- "What would you like to build?"
- "WordPress without the manual."
- "Thinking..." (during load state)
- "Proceed with this action?" (clear, direct)

---

**Overall:** The voice is confident. The tone is human. A few lines try to explain too much. Cut the fat. Let the reader breathe.
